## Q&A

### Q&A-1. With a custom `databaseName`, nothing says where MongoDB processor checkpoints go

Quote: "Every MongoDB `messageStore` ... is built from the client plus those `storage` options" / "Every store keeps processor checkpoints in its own storage".
Consequence: `mongoDBCheckpointer` uses `client.db(undefined)` (`mongoDBCheckpointer.ts:15-31`, `:56-57`, `:103-104`), so checkpoints stay in the default database while events move to `custom_db`; two stores with different databases share checkpoint documents. PostgreSQL's checkpointer follows `migrationOptions.databaseSchemaName` (`postgreSQLProcessor.ts:214-216`, `:233-239`).
Belongs to: qa
Resolution: fixed in r13 by the standing consistency rule (PostgreSQL precedent): checkpoints follow `storage.databaseName`; AC 9.11e.

### Quality-2. Three of the new MongoDB `storage` decisions have no AC
