Free Version

Start Modeling Now!

The free version of prooph board offers a very easy way to get started. No credit card and even no registration required. It is a browser-only version of prooph board. All Cody features are available .

Since the free client has no server connection, cloud storage is disabled and your work is only stored in the browser’s local storage. Use import/export functions to backup your work!
