EventCatalog is open source with a [community edition and commercial plans](/pricing.md). EventCatalog Scale, Enterprise, and commercial integrations are unlocked using **license keys**.

If you are using the community edition, you don't need a license key and can skip this section.

## Where license keys come from[​](#where-license-keys-come-from "Direct link to Where license keys come from")

Scale and integration license keys are managed in [EventCatalog Cloud](https://eventcatalog.cloud). You sign up for an account, activate the plan or integration you want to use, and your license keys appear on your dashboard.

You can then add those keys to your `.env` file (or set them as environment variables) and EventCatalog will unlock the corresponding features.

Enterprise features, including [EventCatalog Federation](/federation.md), use an offline license file. Email [hello@eventcatalog.dev](mailto:hello@eventcatalog.dev?subject=EventCatalog%20Enterprise%20Trial) to request an offline Enterprise trial key.

## Free trials[​](#free-trials "Direct link to Free trials")

Free trials

Scale and every integration come with a **30-day free trial**. No payment is required to start one. Sign in to [EventCatalog Cloud](https://eventcatalog.cloud) and activate what you want to try.

Want to try Enterprise or Federation? Email us at `hello@eventcatalog.dev` and we will send you an offline trial key.

Need longer to evaluate? Email us at `hello@eventcatalog.dev` and we'll happily extend your trial.

## Quick start[​](#quick-start "Direct link to Quick start")

1. Create an account at [eventcatalog.cloud](https://eventcatalog.cloud).
2. Start your [EventCatalog Scale Trial](https://eventcatalog.cloud/dashboard)
3. Pick your [integrations](https://eventcatalog.cloud/dashboard/integrations)
4. Run your catalog with integrations and commercial features are now unlocked.
